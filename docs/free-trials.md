# Free trials on Whop

Plenty of Whop products let you try them for free first. The trial length is shown at checkout, and that number matters more than anything else on the page.

## The rule

Whop's buyer terms (section 6) are clear on what happens when a trial runs out:

> "Free trials that you do not cancel automatically convert to paid subscriptions at the end of the trial period."

and

> "You must cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged."

## What that means in practice

1. Write down the day your trial ends when you start it.
2. If the product is worth keeping, you don't need to do anything: the paid plan starts on its own.
3. If it isn't for you, cancel from your Whop account before that day. The steps are on the [cancelling page](cancelling.md).

The same terms also say some trials are limited to one per person, household or payment method, so a second trial on the same product may not be offered.

Source: [Whop Buyer Terms](https://whop.com/buyer-terms/), section 6. Checked 29 October 2026.
