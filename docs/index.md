# Promo Drop buyer's guide to Whop

Short, sourced notes for anyone about to pay for a membership, course or community on Whop. Every page quotes or links Whop's own documentation and terms, so you can check the original yourself.

Written by the team behind [Promo Drop](https://promodrop.codes), a free site and Chrome extension that lists Whop promo codes tested at Whop's checkout, each with the date it was last checked.

## Pages

- [Free trials](free-trials.md): when a trial turns into a paid plan, and the date to keep in mind.
- [Cancelling a membership](cancelling.md): where the cancel button lives and what happens to your access.
- [What a sale costs the seller](fees.md): Whop's published seller fees, with a dated open dataset.
