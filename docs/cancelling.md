# Cancelling a Whop membership

You cancel from your own Whop account, not by messaging the seller. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it doesn't cut you off straight away.

## On whop.com

1. Sign in at whop.com.
2. Open your profile and go to Orders.
3. Pick the subscription.
4. Choose "Cancel membership".

## In the Whop app

1. Tap your profile picture.
2. Open "Manage Orders".
3. Tap the subscription.
4. Tap "Cancel membership".

## After you cancel

- Access carries on until the end of the period you have already paid for.
- Whop won't bill you again unless you subscribe again.
- A confirmation email arrives once it's done.
- Money for the current period isn't returned automatically. A refund depends on the creator's own refund policy, so read it on the product page before you buy.

Source: [Whop Docs, "Cancel a Subscription"](https://docs.whop.com/memberships-and-access/cancellations-and-refunds/cancel-a-subscription). Checked 29 October 2026.
